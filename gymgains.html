Instructions

  1. Pick the gym you will be training in.
  2. Enter your API key and press "Read"
    • Your API key is stored in your browser and is not sent to any server.
    • Enter or adjust training bonuses for each stat individually.
    • Enter your happy manually or use the calculator for common drugs and boosters.
  3. Enter the total energy you will train per stat. E.g. if you have 1000 energy stacked and are training equally among all four stats, enter 250 in each cell
  4. Press "Train!". Your predicted gains and resulting battle stats will be shown.
  5. If you want to simulate multiple training sessions (e.g. several happy jumps), press the "Copy Predicted Stats to Initial" button.
